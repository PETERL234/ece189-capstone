01 / THE CHALLENGE

When the environment gets uncertain,
perception matters more.

Fog, smoke, and dust can scatter laser light and degrade optical sensing. Radar offers a different perspective, but its measurements can be sparse or noisy. EchoVision investigates how both can work together for safer autonomous navigation.

01 / LiDAR
◎

High-resolution geometry

2D laser scanning provides detailed range and obstacle geometry in clear conditions, but may suffer from scattering and backscatter in fog.

SPATIAL PRECISION
02 / mmWave radar
≋

Resilient sensing

Millimeter-wave measurements help detect objects when optical visibility is compromised, with different resolution and clutter trade-offs.

03 / Fused perception

Complementary strengths

Time-aligned measurements and filtering aim to make obstacle detection and navigation more reliable across changing environments.

Measure the difference.
Not just the promise.

CONTROLLED TEST ENVIRONMENT

Clear air → light fog → dense fog

Planned controlled fog trials use an enclosed test area, an optical fog-opacity measurement, and representative obstacles such as boxes, thin poles, transparent barriers, and moving targets.
